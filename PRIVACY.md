# Focus Reader privacy policy

Focus Reader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing
data. It has no server, no analytics, and no accounts.

The only data it keeps are your own settings: the master on/off switch, per-site
off switches, and three appearance sliders. These are stored in your browser's
extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync` in Chrome, which Chrome may sync
between your signed-in Chrome profiles; Safari keeps them on the device). They
never leave your browser otherwise and are removed when you uninstall the
extension.

The extension reads the text of pages you visit solely to decide which words to
emphasize, entirely on your device. Nothing about those pages is recorded or
sent anywhere.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/jazahn/focus-reader/issues>.
